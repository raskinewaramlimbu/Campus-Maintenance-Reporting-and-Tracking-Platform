FixMyCampus - Reporting Policy (v1.2)

1. Who can use this service
   Any current student or member of staff. You don't need to log in for
   pass-stage functionality, but reports are more useful if you leave
   accurate location details.

2. What happens to your report
   Reports go into a shared queue that Estates staff review each morning.
   Status moves from "New" -> "In Progress" -> "Resolved". You can check
   status any time on the Reports page.

3. Data we keep
   We store the category, location, description, optional photo link and
   timestamps. We do not require your name. See the Privacy page in the
   app for the full policy.

4. Duplicate reports
   If you see an issue that's already been reported, please don't submit
   a duplicate - it slows down triage. Check the Reports list first.
